Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A and T Realtor ("we", "us", "our"), operating the "Cloud Heaven" land project near Igatpuri, Nashik, Maharashtra, India, collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit this website or submit your details to us.

1. Information we collect

When you fill our site-visit form, we collect only the details you choose to give us:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number
  • The city or area where you live
  • Your planned visit time
  • Your budget range

We may also automatically collect basic technical data such as your device type, browser, approximate location (city level), pages viewed and the advertisement you clicked, using cookies and similar technologies. We do not ask for sensitive information such as health details, religion, caste, financial account numbers, Aadhaar or PAN through this website.

2. How we use your information

  • To call or message you about the land project and arrange your site visit
  • To share details of plots, pricing, location and availability that you asked for
  • To understand which advertisements and pages bring genuine enquiries, and to improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

We contact you only about your enquiry and related property information. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share your information with

We share your information only where needed to run our service:

  • Google: Your form answers are stored using Google Forms and Google Sheets. We also use Google Ads and Google's advertising and measurement tools, which may use cookies and similar identifiers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): If you choose to confirm your visit on WhatsApp, your message is sent through WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.
  • Our team and service providers: Our sales executives and trusted service providers (such as site-visit transport or CRM tools) who are bound to keep your data confidential.
  • Authorities: When required by law or a valid legal request.

4. Cookies and advertising

We use Google Ads, which may place cookies on your browser to measure ad performance and show you relevant ads on Google and other websites that partner with Google (including remarketing). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your past visits to this site.

You can control this at any time:

  • Visit Google's Ads Settings to opt out of personalised advertising.
  • Visit aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendor cookies.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed to follow up on your interest and for legitimate business or legal purposes, generally up to 24 months, after which we delete or anonymise them.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as restricted access and secure Google accounts, to protect your information. No online system is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws, you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update it
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time

To do this, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its updated date.
